Why this exists

Right now, nobody can tell you whether last month worked.

You have the tools. You have the numbers. What you don't have is one place where they agree — and one person who has actually read them.

01

Shopify shows sales, not profit

It knows what you sold. It doesn't know what your products cost you, what you paid in ad spend, or what you gave away in discounts. So it can't tell you what you kept.

02

Every ad platform marks its own homework

Meta says it made you money. Google says the same. Add up what they each claim and you get more sales than you actually had. Both can't be right.

03

Dashboards don't tell you what to do

A screen full of charts is not an answer. Somebody still has to look at them, work out what changed, and decide what to do differently this month.

What makes this different

Most tools show you numbers. We tell you what to do about them.

A person actually reads your account

Not an alert. Not a chatbot. Someone looks at your store every month, writes the recommendations, and puts their name on them.

We tell you when nothing needs changing

Some months are fine. We say so instead of inventing three problems. That's why you'll believe us on the months we don't.

Read-only, and no customer data

We never write to your store or touch your campaigns. We don't collect your customers' names, emails or addresses at all — we don't need them.

Every calculation is published

What we mean by "net sales", "new customer" or "profit" is written down on one page you can read. It doesn't change quietly.

Checked against your own tools first

Before a report goes out, our totals are compared to what Shopify and your ad platforms show. Where there's a difference, we explain it in the report.

Priced on ad spend, not your sales

Most tools charge more as your revenue grows, whether or not you use them more. Ours doesn't. Grow all you like — the price stays put.

Common questions

Things people ask before starting.

Do I need to be technical to use this?

No. Setup is installing an app and clicking through two permission screens. After that you receive a PDF and get on a call. There's nothing to learn and nothing to log into unless you want to.

What access do you need, exactly?

Read-only access to your Shopify store and your Google and Meta ad accounts. We can see orders, products and ad spend. We can't change anything, we can't spend your money, and we don't collect your customers' names or email addresses at all.

I already pay for an analytics tool. Why this too?

Those tools show you numbers. This one reads them and tells you what to change. Most people who switch tell us the old dashboard was open twice a month and never led to a decision.

What if your numbers don't match what I see in Shopify?

We check that before you do. Every report is compared against Shopify and your ad platforms first, and where there's a difference we explain it in the report itself. Some differences are normal — Meta revises recent days, for example — and we'd rather tell you than have you find it.

How long until I get something useful?

Your first report lands within about a week of connecting. It's free, and it covers a full month with a call to go through it.

What if I want to stop?

Tell us and we stop. Billing is quarterly, there's no contract, and we'll delete your data on request.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. If you manage ads for several brands, we can report on all of them and you can put your own name on it. Get in touch and we'll work out the pricing.
